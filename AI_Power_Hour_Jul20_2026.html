✅ §9 — Your Action Items This Week

Five concrete things your team can do this week in response to the last two weeks of AI news.

🔍 1. Instrument your agents before you scale

Agent incidents now outpace controls in many enterprises. Before scaling any agent, add identity isolation, scoped permissions, monitoring, and spend caps on high-stakes actions. ↗

💸 2. Negotiate capacity, not just model rates

With multi-billion compute talks now public, the risk is no longer only token price. Add capacity clauses, region fallback, and failover objectives into vendor contracts and architecture reviews. ↗

🛟 3. Make red teaming continuous

Adopt a standing adversarial-testing loop, not periodic reviews. Integrate automated red-team prompts into CI for prompts, tools, and policy boundaries. ↗

🔀 4. Build open-model fallback pathways

Open frontier progress means fallback no longer requires heavy quality tradeoffs. Keep at least one tested open-weight route for critical workflows to reduce lock-in and outage exposure. ↗

🧠 5. Add infra and community risk to deployment plans

Regional pushback can now delay or reshape infrastructure timelines. Include site diversification and policy risk reviews in your scaling roadmap. ↗

⚡ Key Takeaway Two weeks of news, one mandate: reliability over enthusiasm. Instrument your agents, route to the cheap tier, guard vulnerable users, keep a fallback, and protect human skill. The demos are over — the teams that win now are the ones doing the unglamorous, human work of making AI actually dependable.
